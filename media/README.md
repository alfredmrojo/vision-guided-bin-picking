# Showcase media

These visuals accompany the [project showcase](../README.md).

| Visual | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [Perception paths](perception-paths.svg) | A simplified diagram based on the July 2026 report, separating the single-image estimate from the stereo reference and physical motion. |
| [Wrist alignment](wrist-alignment.png) | The final report's schematic of the wrist-mounted camera and coupled position and yaw corrections (Figure 3). |
| [Depth validation](depth-validation.png) | The final evaluation against RealSense and its error distribution (Figure 4, wide layout). |
| [Marker height example](marker-height-example.png) | A development figure showing support-relative marker height from measured, raw and corrected depth, cropped to its first row. Each panel has its own scale. |
| [Surface geometry](surface-geometry.png) | The final height and nearest-surface comparison (Figure 6). |
| [Physical outcomes](physical-outcomes.png) | Trial counts and outcome rates from the final RealSense-guided physical evaluation (Figure 7, wide layout). |
| [Marker grasp](marker-grasp-trial.webp) | A silent, reframed excerpt of approach, grasp and lift from an early fixed-marker bench trial. |

The original setup, calibration and development images remain in the repository. The [depth trend plot](depth-trends.png) belongs to an earlier pixel-level experiment; the main showcase uses the later marker-region and planner-request results from the final report.
